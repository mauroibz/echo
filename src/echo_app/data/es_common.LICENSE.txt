es_common.txt: the 5,000 most frequent words of Spanish subtitles, one per line, most frequent
first. Echo uses it to tell common words from names when learning the dictionary.

Source: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, content/2018/es/es_50k.txt (first 5,000 lines, counts
removed), https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, built from the OpenSubtitles 2018
corpus (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php).
