## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Recorded calls
A recording with a microphone and a computer-audio track SHALL be transcribed from the two tracks
mixed, each at its recorded level. Where speakers are labeled, each passage SHALL be attributed
to the track that is louder in it, speakers SHALL be found within each track separately so a
voice from one track is never merged with a voice from the other, and the microphone's speakers
SHALL be numbered first.

#### Scenario: A two-person call
- **WHEN** a call recorded with computer audio, the user on the microphone and one person on the call, is transcribed
- **THEN** the transcript has both people's speech, the user's passages are speaker 1 and the other person's speaker 2
